## Read first — the missing identity layer in agentic commerce

**Score:** 17/20 (role relevance 5, evidence 3, novelty 5, practical learning 4). **Beat:** secondary platform/identity signal relevant to enterprise customer technology. **Original sources:** [Sierra's 9 October 2026 draft announcement](https://sierra.ai/blog/poppy) and the [Personal Agent Protocol draft](https://personalagentprotocol.org/). **Read:** about 5 minutes.

Sierra and collaborators have published a draft of Personal Agent Protocol, or “Poppy,” to let a consumer's AI agent identify itself, receive bounded permission, and continue a task across a company's website, APIs and agent. The announcement names large enterprises as design partners and describes a Rocket Companies demonstration, but does not show production adoption or commercial impact.

**Why it matters to your work:** This gets to a customer-data and consent question relevant to your Coca-Cola work and to senior MarTech, commerce and transformation roles: when an agent acts for someone, how does the brand know whose intent it represents, what it may do, and how to measure the whole visit?

**Mechanism:** A company publishes a discovery file at `/.well-known/poppy.json` → the personal agent opens a session and identifies itself → the customer signs in on the company's page through OAuth and approves scoped access → one session spans the company's web pages, APIs and agent conversations. The draft uses short-lived signed session tokens, and a company chooses which agents and actions to permit. Sierra positions it alongside commerce protocols that handle product discovery and checkout.

**Outcome and measurement:** Sierra reports a live Rocket mortgage preapproval demonstration with Meta's Muse agent, in which the customer signed in and the two agents produced a preapproval letter. This validates a demonstrated flow, not production reliability, customer adoption, conversion lift or revenue. A future evaluation should measure completed authorized tasks, permission drop-off, revocations, errors and incremental value compared with the human-led journey.

**Read with this question:** What should a brand's consent and identity layer record when an AI agent starts anonymously, then becomes authorized to view or change a customer's account?

**Evidence caveat:** This is a draft from its proponents, announced 9 October. The named enterprises are design participants, not confirmed implementers; the Rocket example is a demo. OAuth and session design are specified, but interoperability and commercial outcomes remain unproven.

**Possible later angle:** Agentic commerce needs a permission and continuity design before it can become a reliable customer journey.

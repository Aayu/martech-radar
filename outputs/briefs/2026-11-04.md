## 1. Read first — Walmart connects shopping intent to the shelf

**17/20: 5 / 3 / 4 / 5.** Enterprise consumer digital / connected shopping journey. [Original Walmart account, 30 October 2026](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/10/sparky-shopping-assistant-expansion). **3 minutes.**

Walmart describes new Sparky image and list inputs alongside in-store guidance. Shop to Light connects an item search to a store map and a flashing shelf label.

**Mechanism:** Shopper searches for an item → map locates its aisle → shopper taps “Flash price tag” → shelf label lights up. Separately, photographed handwritten lists can become shopping carts.

**Why it matters:** Relevant to your omnichannel roles: evaluate whether digital assistance resolves the physical task that follows discovery.

**Outcome / measurement:** No completion, accuracy, conversion or sales-lift result is disclosed.

**Caveat:** Company capability announcement; store-level coverage was not tested. Voice is explicitly expected chainwide only by year-end. Statements about broader access do not establish every capability is available in every market or store.

**Reading question:** Would this reduce failed item searches and improve completed baskets in a controlled store test?

**Possible future angle:** A useful shopping assistant can close the gap between finding an item online and finding it on a shelf.

**Repeat distinction:** Separate from Walmart's already-logged Agent Profiles and Vibe audiences: this is its shopper-facing multimodal and store-guidance release.

## 2. Read next — Smartly puts modeled contribution beside platform reporting

**16/20: 5 / 3 / 4 / 4.** Secondary measurement / media planning. [Original Smartly release, 2 November 2026](https://www.smartly.io/press/smartly-launches-intelligence-pro-to-connect-campaign-planning-performance-and-incrementality). **4 minutes.**

Smartly launched Intelligence Pro for eligible customers. It combines planning and budget-opportunity tools with an additional view of media contribution.

**Mechanism:** Reach/frequency scenarios, headroom analysis and benchmarks → planning decisions; INCRMNTAL Connect adds an incrementality-based signal alongside platform-reported performance.

**Why it matters:** Fits your measurement and transformation lens: compare the evidence behind conflicting budget recommendations within the workflow where decisions happen.

**Outcome / measurement:** Hearst Newspapers supplies a qualitative Headroom testimonial. No quantified effect attributable to this release or validation of the integrated causal model is disclosed.

**Caveat:** Vendor announcement. Calling a signal incremental does not establish experimental causality. Model assumptions, uncertainty, confounding and calibration against holdouts need scrutiny. Boilerplate Smartly-wide ROAS claims are not Intelligence Pro results.

**Reading question:** When platform attribution and modeled contribution disagree, what independent test would resolve the decision?

**Possible future angle:** Adding a measurement view is useful when teams know how to adjudicate disagreement.

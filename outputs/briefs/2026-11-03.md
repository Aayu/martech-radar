## Read first — Coda links creator content to digital-goods purchases

**16/20: 4 / 3 / 4 / 5.** Connected consumer journey / social commerce. [Original Coda account, 30 October 2026](https://www.coda.co/press/coda-tiktok-gaming-1b-views/). **3 minutes.** Launch: mid-June 2026; results described as cumulative since launch. Later syndicated coverage is not the original launch date.

Coda and TikTok Gaming connect creator videos and livestreams to Codashop purchases. The route operates in Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia and the Philippines.

**Mechanism:** Gaming-content discovery → creator features Codashop-listed goods → direct link → game top-up, gift-card or other digital-goods purchase. No personalized decisioning or CRM follow-up is documented.

**Outcome / measurement:** Coda reports one billion video views, average click-through above 5%, around 5% of clicks purchasing, and a majority of buyers new to Codashop. These are company-reported funnel measures, not causal lift.

**Why it matters:** Relevant to your commerce lens: the destination and purchase handoff can be designed alongside creator content. Test whether this recruits incremental buyers rather than moving existing purchases between channels.

**Caveat:** Attribution windows, unique-viewer counts, deduplication and new-buyer definitions are undisclosed. Do not multiply the reported averages into an estimated order total. New to Codashop does not mean new to the game or publisher.

**Reading question:** What holdout would separate new demand from a new route to an existing purchase?

**Possible future angle:** Creator commerce needs a visible purchase path and evidence beyond views.

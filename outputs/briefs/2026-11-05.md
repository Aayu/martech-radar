## 1. Read first — HUL connects availability to quick-commerce conversion

**18/20: 5 / 3 / 5 / 5.** FMCG commerce execution. [Original India presentation](https://www.unilever.com/files/execution-in-market-india-priya-nair.pdf), [4 November investor event](https://www.unilever.com/investors/unilever-investor-event-2026/). **4-minute skim:** PDF pages 14–15 and 18 (one-based PDF numbering).

HUL describes quick-commerce execution from local availability through discovery and conversion. It also shows a traditional-trade loop linking outlet recommendations to ordering.

**Mechanism:** Pin-code/dark-store availability → search and browse visibility → converting content and targeted sampling, supported by an AI optimization engine. Separately, internal/external signals inform outlet recommendations; Shikhar captures retailer demand.

**Outcome / measurement:** HUL reports a 1,400-basis-point improvement in customer-reported fill rate and 1.2× better ROAS, without clear comparators or methodology. It reports quick-commerce underlying sales growth above 50% for SQ'26. None isolates AI's contribution.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant to your Reckitt/PepsiCo commerce lens: media effectiveness depends partly on local product availability and execution ownership.

**Caveat:** Company investor presentation; no control group, engine specification or attribution-window disclosure. Growth and efficiency measures should not be combined into an AI-caused sales claim.

**Reading question:** How would you test whether better availability changes the incremental return from media?

**Possible future angle:** Commerce optimization needs availability and conversion teams working from a shared decision loop.

## 2. Read next — Feedonomics adds a self-service route into Shop Direct

**16/20: 5 / 3 / 4 / 4.** Enterprise commerce distribution / secondary platform signal. [Original Commerce announcement, 4 November 2026](https://investors.commerce.com/news-releases/news-release-details/feedonomics-now-available-amazons-shop-direct-merchant-workspace). **4 minutes.**

Eligible US merchants can select Feedonomics in Amazon's Shop Direct Merchant Workspace. The release describes reusing existing feeds and seeing which products are active.

**Mechanism:** Merchant registers → selects Feedonomics → catalog, price and inventory updates feed Shop Direct → shopper can reach the merchant's storefront and checkout. Products may also be submitted for Amazon's separate Buy for Me route; submission does not establish acceptance or usage.

**Why it matters:** Fits your platform-strategy roles: discovery can occur on a marketplace while checkout and post-purchase responsibilities remain with the merchant.

**Outcome / measurement:** No adoption, referral conversion or incremental-sales result is disclosed.

**Caveat:** Supplier announcement; the Amazon portal failed to open, and onboarding was not tested. Eligibility and any Feedonomics service costs need separate verification. “Free portal” does not establish an entirely cost-free integration.

**Reading question:** What referral, consent and order data would be needed to evaluate the owned-checkout route?

**Possible future angle:** Channel strategy includes deciding who handles discovery, checkout and service.

**Freshness distinction:** Feedonomics syndication already existed in spring 2026. The new element is the self-service Merchant Workspace route, not the first connection to Shop Direct.

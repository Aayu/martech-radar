## 1. Shopify halves the pending window for customer data erasure

**18/20** (role relevance 5, evidence 5, novelty 3, practical value 5). **4 minutes.** Customer-data operations and commerce tooling.

Shopify announced on 11 November that, as of 10 November 2026, customer erasure requests are processed five days after submission. Requests for recipients of scheduled gift cards remain pending until delivery. [Shopify changelog](https://shopify.dev/changelog/posts/updated-handling-of-customer-data-erasure-requests)

A merchant initiates erasure in the customer profile. Shopify describes processing for its store and installed apps and channels, and assigns merchants responsibility for contacting other companies they shared the data with. Its guidance also describes a separate process for data from a customer’s direct relationship with Shop or Shop Pay. The five days refer to Shopify’s workflow, and the release gives no downstream completion data or legal deadline. [Shopify merchant guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/privacy-and-security/privacy/processing-customer-data-requests)

For your customer-data work, this is a concrete example of why an erasure workflow needs named owners for commerce, CRM, analytics and external destinations. **Reading question:** What evidence would show that a request was completed across every system that received the record? **Possible future angle:** How to test whether a commerce erasure request reaches the whole marketing stack.

## 2. JD Sports uses verified student status across its loyalty journey

**16/20** (5, 3, 4, 4). **6 minutes.** Large enterprise and connected journey.

The Drum published the award case on 10 November 2026. It describes how JD Sports embedded SheerID verification in JD STATUS and reused verified eligibility for loyalty offers and communications. JD’s own January 2026 promotion confirms that members could verify in the app and earn a temporary JD Cash boost online and in stores. The case gives no exact start or end date for the wider implementation. [Award case](https://www.thedrum.com/awards-case-study/jd-sports-rebuilt-loyalty-on-verified-student-data-not-discounts) · [JD Sports promotion](https://blog.jdsports.co.uk/jd-status-boosts/)

The customer verifies student eligibility in the loyalty app, the retailer associates the verified attribute with the member profile, and online and in-store purchases can earn the eligible reward. The award case reports higher order value and purchase frequency for verified students than for unverified audiences. It gives no student-specific effect sizes or controlled comparison, so self-selection and reward generosity remain plausible explanations. JD’s reported 10 million active members as of 1 August applies to the whole program. [Award case](https://www.thedrum.com/awards-case-study/jd-sports-rebuilt-loyalty-on-verified-student-data-not-discounts) · [JD half-year results](https://www.jdplc.com/half-year-results-2026-27/)

For lifecycle and CRM roles, this gives you a design problem worth discussing: how a verified attribute is earned, reused and retired when eligibility expires. **Reading question:** How would you test whether verified rewards change repeat purchase after accounting for the customers who choose to verify? **Possible future angle:** Designing an eligibility attribute for the whole loyalty lifecycle.

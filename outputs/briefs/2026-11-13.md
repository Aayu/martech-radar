## 1. India’s Consent Manager registration provisions reach their commencement milestone

**19/20** (role relevance 5, evidence 5, novelty 4, practical value 5). **6 minutes.** Regulation and customer-data governance.

India’s gazette notifications of 13 November 2025 set a one-year commencement period for Rule 4 and Act sections 6(9) and 27(1)(d), yielding 13 November 2026. This milestone covers registration and oversight of Consent Managers. The gazette sets a separate 18-month commencement period for core notice and consent provisions, including Rule 3. [DPDP Rules, Rule 1 and Rule 4](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) · [Act commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf)

Rule 4 provides for eligible applicants to seek registration with the Data Protection Board. The First Schedule requires a Consent Manager to record grants, refusals and withdrawals, relevant notices and certain sharing events, give the person access to those records and retain them for at least seven years. These are statutory design requirements. Operational availability of the application process and any approved providers was unverified in this scan. [DPDP Rules, First Schedule](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)

For your customer-data work, map who owns each consent record and where a withdrawal must be reflected across brand systems and downstream destinations. **Reading question:** What evidence would show that a withdrawal reached the systems that activate an audience? **Future angle:** How to make consent withdrawal observable across a marketing stack.

## 2. Wayfair tests which shopping history belongs in sponsored search

**18/20** (5, 4, 4, 5). **7 minutes.** Large-enterprise customer technology and retail-media measurement. Wayfair ranks 353rd on the 2026 Fortune 500. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/company/wayfair/)

Wayfair researchers published a preprint on 8 November 2026 describing a five-week live sponsored-search experiment. Their model combines recent actions with older actions retrieved for relevance to the current query and to each candidate product. This lets the same customer history supply different signals for different ads within a search. [Wayfair preprint, §§3 and 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.11675v1) · [Submission date](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11675)

Against the production control, the user-randomized test reported 11.5% more orders per impression, a 5.2% increase in attributed conversion rate and 1.3% lower CPC, all relative changes. Randomization strengthens the comparison of ranking systems in the tested traffic. The company-authored preprint reports ad outcomes, leaving total marketplace sales and any diversion from organic listings unresolved. [Wayfair preprint, Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.11675v1#S5)

For digital commerce and retail media, the experiment shows how to test a change in the use of behavioral data while keeping a production comparator. **Reading question:** What primary metric and guardrails would detect a shift from organic orders into sponsored orders? **Future angle:** How to test whether a more relevant sponsored result creates a new sale.

## 3. Diet Coke makes a winning pack redeemable in Nectar

**15/20** (5, 3, 3, 4). **4 minutes.** FMCG and connected campaign. The UK promotion ran 2–22 September 2026, subject to stock. The brand’s terms display the campaign dates and no publication date. [2026 promotion terms, §§6–25](https://www.coca-cola.com/gb/en/legal/purple-can-terms)

Shoppers bought a marked multipack at Sainsbury’s or online and could discover a purple can inside. A phone claim and verification of the winning codes and proof of purchase linked that physical event to 40,000 points credited to an eligible Nectar account. The terms offered 200 prizes, a count of available rewards. Verified redemptions, incremental sales and repeat purchases for this edition were unverified. Nectar360’s similarly named case describes different prize mechanics and cannot establish the 2026 edition’s results. [2026 promotion terms](https://www.coca-cola.com/gb/en/legal/purple-can-terms) · [Nectar360 case study](https://www.nectar360.co.uk/case-studies/nectar-purple-coke-can-campaign/)

For a brand designing with a retailer, the operational trade-off is fraud protection versus the effort of claiming a reward. **Reading question:** Where would you measure drop-off between discovery, verification and credit? **Future angle:** Which steps in a pack-to-loyalty journey need proof of purchase?

## 1. Read first — who controls the identity infrastructure?

**17/20: 5 / 4 / 4 / 4.** Secondary regulation / customer-data governance signal. **19 October 2026**, verified on the [FTC docket](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/publicis-liveramp-matter-timeline-item-2026-10-19). **5 minutes.**

The FTC announced a proposed consent agreement concerning Publicis's acquisition of LiveRamp. It addresses access to advertising-data infrastructure and protection of other customers' information.

**Original sources:** [FTC explanation](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/10/ftc-order-preserves-competition-digital-advertising-ecosystem-part-publiciss-acquisition-liveramp); [order document in the package](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/Publicis-LiveRamp-DO.pdf).

**Mechanism:** Proposed safeguards include neutral access, customer-data firewalls, independent audits and a customer audit-request route. The announcement places the agreement into a 30-day public-comment process. Treat it as a proposal, not a generally applicable effective law or confirmation that the acquisition has closed.

**Why it matters:** For your clean-room and audience work, supplier ownership belongs in architecture and procurement discussions alongside interoperability.

**Outcome / caveat:** Regulatory process evidence; no demonstrated commercial or compliance outcome. The attached document's title alone does not establish final status.

**Reading question:** Which contract, access and audit rights would protect a brand if its identity provider's ownership changed?

**Future angle:** Identity architecture includes governance and commercial dependency.

## 2. Read next — a shopping assistant connected to checkout

**16/20: 5 / 3 / 4 / 4.** Enterprise platform / consumer commerce journey. **4 minutes.** [Original AWS-hosted Therabody announcement](https://press.aboutamazon.com/aws/2026/10/therabody-launches-ai-shopping-assistant-jason-with-aws-to-deliver-personalized-science-backed-recommendations-for-every-body): page dated 22 October; release dateline 21 October 2026.

Therabody says its Jason assistant connects needs-based recommendations and supporting research to shopping actions. Its AWS implementation integrates with Shopify for add-to-bag and checkout from chat.

**Mechanism:** Customer describes needs and constraints → brand-specific context and guardrails → recommendation with sources → basket / checkout. The release describes custom measurement dashboards and a build under 90 days.

**Why it matters:** Concrete implementation questions for commerce leadership include product knowledge, claims approval, escalation, transaction handoff and measurement ownership.

**Outcome / caveat:** A reported launch and build timeline; no conversion lift, incremental revenue, accuracy audit or safety validation disclosed. “Science-backed” remains the brand's description. The assistant was not independently exercised in this run.

**Reading question:** How would you measure recommendation quality and completed purchases against an ordinary product finder?

**Future angle:** A commerce assistant becomes useful when advice, evidence and transaction form a coherent journey.

## 1. Read first — Colgate finds the operational cost of social commerce

**Score:** 17/20 (relevance 5, evidence 3, novelty 4, learning 5). **Beat:** major FMCG / CPG. **Original source:** [firsthand reporting from Colgate-Palmolive's Advertising Week session, 7 October 2026](https://marketersandmoney.com/a-nightmare-for-supply-chain-colgate-braves-tiktok-shop-launches-new-brands-rides-demand-spikes/); [event session](https://newyork2026.advertisingweek.com/aw/schedule/session/-594-2026-10-07-1710-session). **Read:** about 6 minutes.

Colgate-Palmolive says its Hello brand's TikTok Shop test compressed discovery and purchase but produced sharp, unpredictable demand. Its team moved to weekly predictive supply planning and began reviewing TikTok's effects across retailers rather than judging the shop only by its own sales.

**Why it matters to your work:** This is a practical operating-model example for Reckitt or PepsiCo digital commerce and McKinsey-style transformation roles: the platform choice changes supply planning, creative approval, budget ownership and measurement together.

**Mechanism:** Platform-native content and algorithmic product discovery → TikTok Shop demand → faster forecast cycle and fulfillment response → purchases on TikTok or another digital or physical retailer → cross-channel measurement and budget decisions. Colgate's manager says the team had to move from quarterly to weekly predictive modeling and reported off-platform effects, while its measurement partner argues for a planned cross-channel measurement framework.

**Outcome and measurement:** The article reports Colgate's observation of purchases across digital and physical retail, but gives no sales increment, control group or quantified lift. The claim that TikTok generated those off-platform purchases is a practitioner interpretation; the article does not establish causality or explain the analytics method.

**Read with this question:** If a social shop accelerates demand and some purchases move to retailers, which leading indicators should feed supply planning, and what holdout would prove the channel's incremental value?

**Possible later angle:** A new commerce channel is an operating-model decision when it changes demand volatility, inventory and who gets credit for a sale.

## 2. Read next — Target turns a holiday catalog into a shared shopping path

**Score:** 15/20 (relevance 4, evidence 3, novelty 4, learning 4). **Beats:** large enterprise technology; connected campaign. **Original source:** [Target announcement, 5 October 2026](https://corporate.target.com/news-features/article/2026/10/target-holiday-kids-catalog); [live catalog](https://www.target.com/catalog?brandId=lego&pageId=1&ref=index&type=holiday). **Read:** about 4 minutes.

Target's 2026 holiday toy catalog joins a printed mailer with its app and site: page-specific QR codes and a handwritten-list scanner carry families into the digital catalog. There, creator videos and demonstrations support discovery, while shared wish lists and direct cart actions let different gift-givers continue the journey.

**Why it matters to your work:** This is a concrete design pattern for consumer digital and commerce roles: a physical touchpoint becomes a persistent, collaborative shopping intent signal instead of a one-time visit.

**Mechanism:** Printed page or handwritten wish list → QR or app scan → corresponding digital page or list → video-assisted evaluation → saved and shared wish list → cart. The wish list records product interest within the shopping experience; Target has not disclosed whether that signal drives subsequent personalization or CRM.

**Outcome and measurement:** Target discloses the experience design, including nearly 500 products, but no usage, conversion or sales result for the new catalog. A useful measurement plan would track scan-to-list, list sharing, list-to-cart and purchase, with a comparison group to test whether the connected experience changes sales.

**Read with this question:** Which moment should be measured as the campaign's real contribution: a scan, a shared wish list, or an incremental purchase by a gift-giver?

**Evidence caveat:** This is Target's launch description. The live catalog page opens, but its interactive content is not exposed in the text view; the detailed feature mechanics above come from Target's own announcement. No impact claim is made.

**Possible later angle:** The useful role of a QR code is to preserve context between physical discovery and a decision someone can complete later.
